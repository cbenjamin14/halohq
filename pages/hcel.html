Halo: Combat Evolved — Remake Rumors (Unreal Engine 5)

Release Date: TBA (rumors surfaced October 15, 2025)

Halo: Combat Evolved fans are buzzing over multiple reports claiming the original 2001 classic is receiving a full remake built in Unreal Engine 5. Overview:

Multiple outlets and community leaks indicate Halo Studios (the rebranded 343 Industries organization) is moving key IP work to Unreal Engine 5 and that a Combat Evolved remake is in early development or being shopped to co-development partners. Some reports mention a full asset rebuild and modernized gameplay systems, while others describe a faithful visual overhaul preserving the original pacing and encounter design.

Highlights (Rumored):

A full Unreal Engine 5 remake of Halo: Combat Evolved could redefine how Halo’s legacy is presented — uniting nostalgic gameplay with cutting-edge graphics, reworked cinematics, and new accessibility options. It would also mark Halo Studios’ first large-scale test of their new UE5 pipeline.
